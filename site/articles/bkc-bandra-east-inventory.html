BKC and Bandra East: what brokers advertised in the last 7 days

Asking rents and prices for homes quoted in Mumbai broker WhatsApp groups between 18 and 24 November 2026, for buildings around BKC and Bandra East.

How to read this

These are asking quotes that brokers posted in WhatsApp broker groups. They are not confirmed availability, sale prices or agreed rents, and a home quoted here may already be let or sold.

The window runs from 18 November 2026 to the latest captured post, at about 9:54 PM IST on 24 November. Anything posted after that, and any group that was not captured, is missing, so this is a sample of the market and not all of it.

The same flat is often reposted and advertised by several brokers, so the number of posts says nothing about how many homes exist, and this article gives no counts of homes. The ranges below come from individual quotes that named one of these buildings, a bedroom count, and a rent or price. Buy-side requirement posts, commercial listings and buildings in Bandra West, Khar and Santacruz are left out. Broker names, contacts and group names are left out too.

Sizes are shown in sq ft exactly as quoted. Most quotes said carpet, some said RERA carpet and some gave no basis, so sizes from different quotes are not strictly comparable. A few prices say they include stamp duty or are all-inclusive, and many say negotiable.
