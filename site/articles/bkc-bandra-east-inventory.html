Comparing five BKC and Bandra East projects: what a week of asking prices shows

If you are looking for a flat near Bandra Kurla Complex, your budget and the size you need can point to different buildings. This piece compares five projects using broker quotes from 18 to 24 November 2026.

The five selected projects are Adani TEN BKC (also marketed as X BKC) in Kala Nagar, Forum Serendipity in BKC, Kalpataru Magnus in Bandra East, Rustomjee Seasons in the BKC Annexe, and Kanakia Paris in BKC. This piece goes through them in the order a renter or buyer would meet them. Everything here comes from asking quotes posted in Mumbai broker WhatsApp groups, so it describes what was being asked, not what was agreed or what is available today.

If you are renting a 3 BHK

Three projects had enough 3 BHK rent quotes to compare. Kalpataru Magnus asked ₹2.55 to 3.50 lakh a month for flats of 1,150 to 1,321 sq ft. Adani TEN BKC asked ₹2.75 to 3.70 lakh, with sizes of 1,222 to 1,451 sq ft where they were stated. Forum Serendipity asked ₹3.75 to 4.25 lakh, and every one of its 3 BHK quotes was for a flat of about 1,465 to 1,482 sq ft.

So if your budget is around ₹3 lakh a month, Magnus and TEN BKC were the projects with quotes in reach that week, and Forum's quotes started above it. If you can stretch to ₹4 lakh, Forum comes into play, and you would be looking at larger flats than the Magnus quotes. Some of the Magnus quotes were marked negotiable, and a few TEN BKC quotes were fully or semi-furnished, so ask what a number includes before comparing it with another.

One oddity is worth knowing about. A 1,600 sq ft 4 BHK at TEN BKC was quoted at ₹3.20 lakh, below several of that building's 3 BHK quotes. It was a single quote, so it is a reason to ask about bigger flats, not a pattern.

If you want something smaller

Below ₹2 lakh a month, the quotes were for 2 BHKs and smaller. TEN BKC had 2 BHKs from ₹1.80 lakh for 741 sq ft, and Magnus had one at ₹1.95 lakh for 781 sq ft. Further into Bandra East, Leo Eminence quoted ₹1.30 lakh for a 2 BHK and ₹0.90 lakh for a 422 sq ft 1 BHK.

If you are buying

Buyers can think of three steps. At the entry point, Kanakia Paris asked ₹4.10 crore for an 850 sq ft 2 BHK and ₹6.00 to 6.50 crore for a 1,070 sq ft 3 BHK, and Jade Gardens in Bandra East quoted a 1,000 sq ft 3 BHK at ₹5.95 crore. TEN BKC's 2 BHKs of 741 to 812 sq ft were quoted at ₹4.45 to 5.75 crore, and one of those prices was marked all-inclusive.

In the middle, Rustomjee Seasons had a 1,144 sq ft 3 BHK at ₹7.29 crore. Then Kalpataru Magnus asked ₹8.50 to 9.00 crore for 3 BHKs of 1,321 to 1,374 sq ft, and Forum Serendipity ₹8.51 to 9.50 crore for 1,465 to 1,482 sq ft. Those two overlap in price while Forum's quotes were for the larger flats, which makes size on the same measurement the thing to compare.

TEN BKC's 3 BHK sale quotes covered a wide range of quoted size, from ₹6.25 crore for 985 sq ft to ₹9.90 crore for 1,415 sq ft, so ask for the size before comparing prices.

For larger homes, Rustomjee Seasons was the only project with 4 BHK sale quotes in this sample, at ₹11.39 to 11.93 crore for 1,710 to 1,711 sq ft and one at ₹18 crore for 1,923 sq ft. It also had a 4 BHK rent quote of ₹4.25 lakh a month.

How to read these numbers

Most quotes gave carpet area, some said RERA carpet and some gave no basis, so sizes are not strictly comparable from one quote to the next. Some sale prices include stamp duty or are described as all-inclusive, and many rents and prices say negotiable. The same flat is often reposted, sometimes by different brokers at different numbers, so the number of posts says nothing about how many homes exist, and this article gives no counts.

The sample has limits too. It covers posts from 18 November 2026 to about 9:54 PM IST on 24 November in the groups that were captured, and anything outside that is missing. It leaves out buy-side requirements, commercial listings, Bandra West, Khar and Santacruz, and names no brokers or groups. One week cannot say whether prices are rising or falling, so it does not try to.
